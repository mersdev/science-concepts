# Experiment illustration prompt set

All 18 new images were generated with the built-in imagegen tool as transparent PNGs. Character scenes referenced `mascot-base.png` and `01-boyle.png`; apparatus icons referenced `assets/experiments/boyle.png` and `assets/experiments/convex-lens.png`.

## Shared character prompt

Create one standalone transparent sticker illustration, no text, no border, no backdrop, no white sticker edge. Reference image 1 is the exact established mascot identity: curled dark teal hair, coral skin and round cheeks, big oval eyes, white lab coat with teal pocket and shirt, teal trousers, short limbs, rounded proportions. Reference image 2 is the exact finished series style: smooth bold dark teal outlines, clean teal/coral/paper-white palette, subtle gentle shading. Preserve that character. Every prop must use the same simple rounded cartoon style, never realistic, metallic, glossy or finely detailed. Clear friendly educational action, full character and prop entirely inside frame with transparent margins.

### Character scene subjects

- Tower: carefully testing a short, broad-based stack of colorful blocks on a little platform.
- Bridges: placing a small load on a teal triangular truss bridge spanning two supports.
- Seesaw: balancing small coral and teal weights on opposite ends of a seesaw with a central fulcrum.
- Gears: turning two clearly meshing teal and coral toothed gears with a small crank.
- Wedge: operating a coral-handled wedge in a small guided press to separate two golden sample halves.
- Inclined plane: pushing a small loaded trolley up a teal inclined ramp onto a low platform. The final scene uses one golden cube as its load.
- Screw: using a simple screwdriver to turn a large screw into a small wood block.
- Pencil sharpener: turning a coral hand crank on a small teal cutaway pencil sharpener with one pencil and visible gears.
- Scissors and hole punch: comparing rounded coral scissors with a compact teal hole punch and a small cream paper sheet.

## Shared apparatus icon prompt

Create one isolated apparatus icon for a science experiment. Match the supplied icon reference images exactly: friendly simplified cartoon, thick rounded dark-teal outlines, teal/sage/coral/cream palette, clean flat fills with very restrained soft shading. No scientist or character, no words, no text, no border, no background. Actual alpha transparency around and between objects. Compact centered apparatus, fully inside square canvas with generous margins, readable at small sizes.

### Apparatus icon subjects

- Tower: a wobbly stack of five chunky colorful blocks on a small flat base, with two tiny wobble marks; no press or gauge.
- Bridges: a triangular teal truss bridge with supports and a small coral load.
- Seesaw: a balanced beam with coral and sage weights on a central fulcrum.
- Gears: two meshing gears with a small crank.
- Wedge: a simple wedge separating two halves of a golden sample.
- Inclined plane: a small loaded cart on a teal ramp leading to a raised platform.
- Screw: a screw in a block with rotation arrows.
- Pencil sharpener: a hand-crank sharpener with an exposed cutting roller and gears.
- Scissors and hole punch: both tools with a paper sheet and visible punched holes.
