# Nervous system / Sistem saraf / 神经系统

Open `index.html` directly, offline. Choose English, Bahasa Melayu or 中文. Explore seeing a cup, withdrawing a hand from heat, and automatic heartbeat regulation. Select the brain, spinal cord or peripheral nerves to read a short explanation.

Send a message animates one journey. Auto explore cycles the three examples; Pause stops it. Scrub 0–100% with the slider, drag the model or use arrow keys (5%), Home and End. Reset restores the cup example and 0%. Full Screen retains the model, current explanation and draggable hand indicator. Escape exits and restores focus.

The model separates sensory input, central processing and motor output. A withdrawal reflex is coordinated by the spinal cord, with information also reaching the brain. The heartbeat has an intrinsic pacemaker and is modulated by autonomic nerves; it does not require a fresh brain instruction for every beat.

This is a classroom anatomical teaching board, not an anatomically scaled body. Nerve branches are illustrative, not individual named nerves; routes and timing are simplified. Animation percentage is lesson progress, never milliseconds or a measure of reaction speed. Heat is a virtual example only. No real hot object is needed. The three heartbeat stages illustrate regulation rather than the origin of each heartbeat.

Parts: base and its three faces; two feet; backing and inset; four fasteners and slots; torso; head; brain hemispheres and six folds; spinal trunk and thirteen segments; fourteen peripheral branches and three limb traces; upper arm, forearm, shoulder joint, elbow joint, hand, motor trace; cup body, rim and handle; heat pad and three heat marks; message marker. More than 60 purposeful elements, painted from supporting board to anatomy and moving arm.

The light palette and compact workspace follow the secondary experiments. All motion and text share one state model. Motion pauses while hidden or offscreen; reduced motion advances directly to the final explanation. No dependencies or build step.

Run `node check.mjs` from the repository root. See [repository README](../../README.md).
