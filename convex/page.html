The refracted rays actually meet: this is a real image that can be projected onto a screen.

Focal length, f is the distance from the optical centre to the principal focus, where rays parallel to the principal axis converge.

A real image is formed where rays meet. A virtual image is formed where the rays appear to come from; it cannot be caught on a screen.

THREE POSITIONS. THREE STORIES.

Cross the focus.
Change the image.

The sign of v tells you which side of the lens holds the image.

01

Outside the focus

u > f gives v > 0. Rays converge to form a real, inverted image. At u = 2f, the image is at 2f and the same size.

Further away → v approaches f
02

Inside the focus

u < f gives v < 0. Refracted rays diverge; their backward extensions meet to form an upright, magnified virtual image.

At u = f → parallel rays, image at infinity
