ONE EQUATION. TWO STORIES.

Why does the bulb
bend the rules?

Ohm’s law needs a constant temperature.
A glowing filament has other plans.

01

A steady resistor

With temperature constant, resistance stays the same. Each extra volt produces the same increase in current.

V ∝ I   →   constant gradient
02

A warming filament

More current heats the filament. Stronger lattice vibrations make resistance rise, so current increases less rapidly.

R rises   →   a curved V–I graph
